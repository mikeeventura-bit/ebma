Black
Radish

Our longest-running program. Black Radish sources low-cost groceries from predominantly Black distributors and regional farmers, then gets them directly to the doors of hungry New Yorkers.

  • Food sourced from Black distributors and New York farmers
  • Serving Ocean Hill, Brownsville, East New York, Cypress Hills, Breukelen Houses and Starrett City
  • Every purchase moves money back into the community food system

Where we started

It started with neighbors helping neighbors.

During COVID-19, East Brooklyn residents faced extraordinary challenges accessing food and basic resources. EBMA organized. Neighbors delivered groceries. Volunteers mobilized. Farmers and community organizations became partners.

What began as an emergency response revealed something larger: East Brooklyn needed stronger, community-controlled food infrastructure. That realization shaped the organization EBMA is becoming today.
