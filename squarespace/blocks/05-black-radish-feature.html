Black
Radish

Our longest-running program. Black Radish sources low-cost groceries from predominantly Black distributors and regional farmers, then gets them directly to the doors of hungry New Yorkers.

  • Food sourced from Black distributors and New York farmers
  • Serving Ocean Hill, Brownsville, East New York, Cypress Hills, Breukelen Houses and Starrett City
  • Every purchase moves money back into the community food system
